# Screenshots

Evidence for defect reports and test runs.

Naming: `BUG-001-invoice-total.png`, `API-run-summary.png`, `ES-01-uom-conversion.png`.

Crop to the relevant area and annotate the point of interest. A full-screen screenshot with no indication of what to look at is not evidence.

**Before committing:** check every image for anything you would not want public — real names, email addresses, licence keys, anything from a previous employer's systems.
